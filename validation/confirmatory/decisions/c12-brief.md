# Decision C12 - monolith vs microservices for a new product

An 8-engineer team is starting a **new SaaS product** from scratch, pre-product-market-fit, expecting rapid iteration and pivots. Choose the initial architecture:

- **A) Modular monolith** (one deployable, clear internal module boundaries).
- **B) Microservices** from day one (independent services + infra).

Commit to A or B, name the single biggest risk, and give the cheapest test/criterion that would tell them they chose wrong.
