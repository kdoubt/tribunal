### 1. ATTACK

**Three rounds by default, optional fourth.** This is ceremony priced as rigor. Independent analysis → targeted rebuttal already yields retained/changed/unresolved if you require those labels in the rebuttal. A mandatory “revision under challenge” round exists to produce labeled deltas that a schema could have demanded in round 2. An optional fourth “tie-break” is not a panel round; it is a test, source check, or critic. Defaulting to three substantive model rounds pays for restatement. Stop rule is stated, then contradicted by the default.

**Blinded relay as a legitimate mode.** Heterogeneity is useful *because* vendor identity is a bias prior (tool-shaped conservatism vs. punch). Stripping attribution at judgment time discards the discount function. Style deanonymizes anyway in a two-CLI panel, so you get the bias without the correction. Traceability argument for attributed debate is right; the exception undoes it.

**“If evidence does not resolve, choose using explicit decision criteria.”** That is the synthesizer casting a vote after the seats failed to. Decision criteria belong to the human or to a cheap oracle (test, compiler, primary doc). An orchestrator “choice” here is authority laundering with extra steps—the failure mode their own §3 names.

Narrower miss: rebuttal of *the* strongest claim and *the* most consequential error. High-impact reviews have several load-bearing claims. One-error targeting lets the rest survive.
